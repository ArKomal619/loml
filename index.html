My dear Arko,

I wanted to make you something small, the way you make ordinary days feel a little softer just by being in them. There isn't a big occasion for this — that's sort of the point. Some feelings are worth writing down even on an unremarkable Thursday.

You have a way of making things feel steadier. Easier to laugh in, easier to be myself in. I don't think I say that enough, so consider this me saying it properly, in writing, where it can't be forgotten.

Thank you for being patient with me, for choosing me again on the ordinary days, and for making me feel so completely loved. I hope this made you smile, even just a little.

Always yours, with love
